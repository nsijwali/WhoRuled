## Hacker News (Show HN)
**Show HN: WhoRuled, an interactive map of who ruled where, 1000 BC to today**
I built a free map with a year slider: pick a year, click a state, see its rulers and government with
sources. It is a static site (Astro, one React island, Canvas renderer), borders from historical-basemaps and
Natural Earth, leaders from Wikipedia/Wikidata with provenance on every claim. The look changes with the era
(cave wall, manuscript, atlas, newsprint). Known gaps: leader data is thin before 1700, and borders before 1880
are approximate (drawn dashed). Corrections welcome, each state has a "Suggest a correction" button.
